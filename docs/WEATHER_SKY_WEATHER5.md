## Sound provenance

The 23 shipped WAVs are original procedural VASC samples generated by `tools/build_weather_audio.py`; exact hashes remain in `assets/audio/weather/SOURCES.json`. They include rain, roof rain, wind, snow wind, three thunders and four variants each of snow/splash/damp/wet-grass steps. This candidate changes the rain mixer, not those source recordings.

External candidates found and their page licences checked on 2026-09-28, **not downloaded, auditioned or incorporated**:
