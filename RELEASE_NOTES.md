# Voxel Ascendant 3.0.61 — Halloween hat hotfix

Halloween witch hats now fit the actual rendered NPC instead of covering the face or floating above the hair. The brim sits in the upper hair, scales to the figure and follows its camera orientation, breathing, walking offsets and seating transform. Hat size stays stable through walking poses.

Complete update based on public 3.0.60. Existing Halloween timing, NPC selection and player/Pokemon exclusions are retained. Replace the mod folder and keep saves/settings; restart the game to load the update.

Native macOS checks with Voxel and Cobble characters, multiple camera views and calendar OFF passed. Contact/face-clearance, calendar and atlas-loading regressions passed. Physical mobile devices were not tested.
