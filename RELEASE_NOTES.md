# Voxel Ascendant 3.0.56

- Fixes temporary 2D fallbacks during map changes and before battles. Known scene preparation stays behind the loading screen until ready; movement cannot advance an unseen world.
- Gate preparation includes the connected outdoor region and its immediate visual border. This repairs missing surroundings on the Route 12–15 corridor, including Route 13. Memory limits still apply.
- Bundles the Gen 1 Cobble character Card. F6 and the character selector can use Cobble without a separate Card; Red, Blue and Green keep their identities. Jessie, James and Officer Rocky are included. HD human artwork remains optional DLC.
- Fixes the crash when opening “Your Look.”
- Restores Cobblemon selection for town, ambient, wild and follower Pokémon through F3; these controls no longer require the optional HD pack. Changing a source refreshes existing Pokémon.
- Restores the seated pose on chairs and benches. The Game Corner aisle-exit fix remains included.
- Moves the Route 12 lookout’s Pokémon Tower binoculars to the opposite railing and corrects their interaction direction. The fishing binoculars keep their view.
- Adds a localized closed-shop interaction at the decorative shop on the right of Celadon’s middle row, in voxel mode.
- Adds a one-time, per-save teleport Easter egg in English/German, before the first real voxel teleport. Ordinary doors are unaffected.

Update the existing VASC installation with this complete ZIP and restart. No new save is needed. KASC 6.7.29 remains compatible and unchanged.

Desktop runtime tests and limitations are in QA-REPORT.md. Physical iPhone/Switch performance has not been certified; this hotfix does not promise constant 60 FPS or eliminate every first-use stall.
